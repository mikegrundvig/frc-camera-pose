Put your robot on the field, place its AprilTag cameras, and see what they see from here and how much of the field they cover.

Show

Drag the robot to move it and the round handle to turn it. It stops at the elements, the walls and other robots. With the field selected, hold W A S D or the arrow keys to drive and Q and E to turn; Shift is slow.

Cameras

Position in from the robot's center on the floor: forward, left, up. Yaw turns left from straight ahead; tilt is up from level.

Cameras behind the bumpers always look over part of the robot. Tighten this, or use a camera's yaw limits, if something on your robot stands at camera height. Views that cross further are left out of Find a layout and Best angles, and a camera whose view does is flagged.

Find a layout

Searches for the cameras that give this field the lowest 95% position error: one to six, on the robot's edges (its four corners and four side middles), each with its own yaw, height and tilt. Every camera uses the lens of the camera selected now.

Best angles

Keeps every camera where it is (its forward, left and up in the Cameras section) and finds the yaw and tilt of each that give this field the lowest 95% position error. Each camera's yaw and tilt limits, in its card, say which directions are blocked. The search does not know your robot's own mechanisms.

Other robots

Solid boxes the size of your robot that hide the tags behind them. Add one, then drag it on the field and turn it by its round handle. Select one and press Delete, or use Remove, to take it off.

Noise

The defaults are the camera placement study's: a quarter pixel (in the camera's image) of noise on each tag corner, the gyro a quarter degree off, tags detected up to . The distance cutoff is one robot's rule (its localizer ignores a camera's measurement when that camera's tags average farther than this, there); teams' estimators differ.
